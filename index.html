Escrow the budget once.
Release it stage by stage.

The client locks the whole budget in the contract, then defines milestones with their own acceptance criteria and payout amounts. The contractor submits each deliverable, an LLM jury reads it on-chain against those criteria, and on approval the client releases that stage's GEN. Whatever isn't released stays under custody until termination returns it.
